Slog Sweep: privacy policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Who we are

Slog Sweep is a single-player cricket card game published by Digjoy Nandi. Contact: digjoynandi12@gmail.com.

What the game stores

The game has no accounts and asks for no name, email address or other personal details.

Your progress (the run in progress, unlocked Tournaments, and your music, sound and vibration settings) is saved in a file on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising

The game shows rewarded ads only when you choose to watch one (for an extra Ball, a free Reroll or sponsor cash). Ads are served by Google AdMob.

To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process data from your device, including the advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. We do not receive this data in a form that identifies you. How Google uses it is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the game asks for your consent, through Google's consent form, before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time: open Settings in the game and press "Privacy choices". The button is shown only where a consent form applies.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device's settings.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and its store listing is for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
